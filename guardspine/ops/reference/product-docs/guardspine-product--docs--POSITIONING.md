# GuardSpine Positioning Guide v1.0

## Core Thesis

"Architecture rots because context rots."

## One-Liner

GuardSpine is the evidence substrate for AI-speed production -- and the compression layer that keeps humans in control as velocity scales.

## Why Now

AI increases bricklaying rate. That means drift accelerates. Companies need automation-native governance substrate because humans cannot maintain vigilance at AI velocity.

## Category

Entropy control for AI-accelerated production. NOT "governance platform." NOT "compliance tool." NOT "code review."

## Key Messages

1. Every change becomes verifiable context + compressed decisions
2. Scale velocity without drowning in audits
3. AI proposes, humans dispose (never bypasses humans)
4. Offline verifiable (auditors verify without trusting the vendor)
5. Governing rule sets is the real organizational problem (meta-governance)

## Trust Inversion Narrative

Before GuardSpine: governance = friction (pre-approval, blocking, slowdown)

After GuardSpine: governance = enabler (post-hoc evidence, compressed decisions, audit-ready)

The inversion: more governance = more velocity (because context is captured automatically)

## Anti-Patterns (What We Are NOT)

- NOT a linter (we produce evidence, not warnings)
- NOT a CI tool (we wrap CI with provenance)
- NOT just for code (PDF, Sheet, Image lanes)
- NOT vendor-locked (open spec, offline verify)
