# Chris Hood Meeting Runsheet

**10-Minute Spine - Don't Overshoot, Don't Under-Ask**

---

## Minute 0-2: Re-Anchor Alignment

**Say this (almost verbatim):**

> "Last time we discovered strong alignment on linguistics, governance, and the 'governed by accident' problem. I've since mapped GuardSpine directly to your Nomotic architecture. I want to propose something concrete: Nomotic Mode."

**Goal:** "We're aligned on the problem; here's the concrete thing."

**Don't:** Rehash the call. He remembers.

---

## Minute 2-6: Present Nomotic Mode

**Show only two things:**

### 1. The Corrected Mapping Table (1 slide or verbal)

| Nomotic Layer       | GuardSpine (What Ships)                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Authority Layer     | Policy Packs + Rubrics + Authority Model           |
| Control Plane       | Gates + "Stop-the-line" enforcement                |
| Governed Adaptation | Governance-change workflow (govern the governance) |
| Evidence & Trust    | Hash-chained bundles + offline verification        |

### 2. The Board Packet Workflow (verbal walkthrough)

> "Quarterly deck + KPI spreadsheet + policy PDF. GuardSpine diffs all three, Nomotic Mode fires rules like 'external link added requires L3 review' or 'finance formula changes require Finance approver.' Bundle gets exported with 'what changed, who approved, why.' Offline verifier proves it without trusting us."

**Do:**

- Let him interrupt (good sign)
- Answer questions briefly
- Point to the scope limitation: "Nomotic governs authorization and evidence, not model behavior"

**Don't:**

- Walk every principle
- Read the tables aloud
- Defend edge cases unless asked

---

## Minute 6-9: Ask the Binary Question

**Say exactly this:**

> "Would you rather start with Nomotic Mode as an informally reviewed rubric pack, or do you want to explore a Certified tier with formal approval rights?"

**Why this works:**

- Forces a decision without pressure
- Signals you're ready to ship
- Avoids "let's keep talking" limbo

**Handle responses:**

| He Says                          | You Do                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Mode sounds good"               | WIN - proceed to next step                                                                   |
| "Let me think about Certified"   | Still a WIN - means he's engaged                                                             |
| "Hmm, I'm not sure"              | Ask "What would you need to see?"                                                            |
| "What's the difference exactly?" | Explain: "Mode = you review informally, we ship. Certified = you formally approve, we wait." |

---

## Minute 9-10: Lock ONE Next Step

**Don't offer three options. Pick one:**

**If Mode:**

> "I'll draft `nomotic-core.yaml` and send it for your review. One week?"

**If Certified exploration:**

> "Let's schedule a 30-minute call to discuss brand usage rules. I'll send calendar options."

**If hesitation:**

> "How about I send you the Board Packet workflow as a 1-pager, and you tell me if it resonates with your audience?"

**Then stop talking.** Let him commit.

---

## Emergency Counters (Only If Asked)

| He Probes                         | You Say                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Is this post-hoc audit?"         | "No, we block merge/export/share. It's in-path, not after-the-fact."                                |
| "Will AI misrepresent Nomotic?"   | "All Nomotic content is direct citation from your materials. No AI summarization without approval." |
| "What if I change my mind later?" | "Certified is opt-in. Mode can stay informal forever if you prefer."                                |
| "Who else are you talking to?"    | "You're the only governance framework we're aligning with. Nomotic is the cleanest fit."            |

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't show the whole brief document
- Don't walk all 5 principles
- Don't mention revenue share / equity / legal yet
- Don't offer to "support his book launch" as the primary value prop
- Don't overstay if he says yes early

---

## Success Criteria

The meeting is a success if you leave with ONE of these:

1. **"Send me the rubric pack"** (Mode)
2. **"Let's schedule the Certified discussion"** (Certified)
3. **"Send me that workflow example"** (Interest, needs more)

Anything else = keep it warm, follow up in 2 weeks.

---

## Post-Meeting Actions (Depending on Outcome)

| Outcome            | Next Action                                                     | Timeline  |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Mode confirmed     | Draft `nomotic-core.yaml`, send for review                      | 5-7 days  |
| Certified interest | Send brand usage proposal, schedule 30min                       | 3-5 days  |
| Wants to think     | Send Board Packet 1-pager, wait                                 | 2 weeks   |
| Unclear / vague    | Send thank-you, propose "let's reconnect when timing is better" | 2-4 weeks |

---

## The One-Liner (Meeting Close)

If things go well, land this:

> "Nomotic creates governance demand. GuardSpine satisfies it at the artifact level. Let's ship Mode v1."
