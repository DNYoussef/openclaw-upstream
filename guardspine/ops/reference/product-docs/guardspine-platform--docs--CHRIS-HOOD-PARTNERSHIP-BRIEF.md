## Key Alignment Points (From Jan 15 Call)

Based on your previous conversation, these themes have **confirmed mutual resonance**:

### 1. "Governed by Accident" Thesis

- **Chris's view:** Most AI deployments stumble into governance retroactively
- **GuardSpine:** Governance is pre-baked - every artifact change goes through the spine

### 2. Linguistics and AI Governance

- **Chris's view:** Language precision matters for governance (hence "Nomotic" from Greek _nomos_)
- **GuardSpine:** Deterministic diffs, hash-verified claims, auditability as first-class concern

### 3. Risk Patterns in Unaccountable Automation

- **Chris's view:** Silent failures before they become regulatory/political problems
- **GuardSpine:** L0-L4 graduated risk tiers, automatic escalation, approval workflows

### 4. Fiduciary Obligation (Chris quote: "AI governance is no longer a technology issue - it is a fiduciary obligation")

- **GuardSpine answer:** Evidence bundles that auditors accept, compliance-grade artifact trails
