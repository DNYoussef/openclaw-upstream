## QUICK REFERENCE: COMMON INVESTOR QUESTIONS

**Q: How do you get to $1M ARR?**
A: Blended: 30 Starter ($15K MRR) + 8 Team ($16K MRR) + 2 Org ($24K MRR) = $55K MRR -> $660K ARR Year 1. Starter is the volume wedge; Team/Org upgrades push toward $1M by month 15-18.

**Q: How many months of runway from the $1M raise?**
A: Runway is not the right frame. At 98% margins we hit breakeven in 4-11 months depending on scenario (bear M11, base M5, bull M4). We burn only $29K-$206K of the $1M to get there. The rest is safety buffer and growth capital. We do not need another raise to survive.

**Q: What's the gross margin story?**
A: 98%+ gross margin on all tiers. BYOK (bring your own keys) = minimal infrastructure costs. Revenue scales, COGS doesn't. At $100K MRR we have $62.5K/mo available for sales hires -- revenue funds growth, not VC.

**Q: What happens if we raise more?**
A: We may not need to. Each funding round dilutes founders ~15-25%. At 45% ownership (pre-seed only), David needs $111M exit for $50M. At 27% (post-Series A), he needs $185M. More dilution = higher bar. We raise again only if the growth rate justifies it, not because we need the money.

**Q: What's the exit scenario?**
A: Capital-efficient path: $111M exit at 45% ownership = $50M per founder, achievable at $9.3M ARR (12x multiple). With post-breakeven hiring (6 specialists): base reaches $9.3M ARR at M24, bull at M16. At $1B: $450M per founder at 45% (vs $190M at 19% after full VC dilution). Second-wave hiring from excess cash accelerates beyond these numbers.

**Q: When do you need Series A?**
A: Maybe never. At 98% margins, revenue self-funds growth after month 5. A small acceleration round ($500K-$1M for 8-10% dilution) is the most we would consider, and only from a position of profitability. Traditional Series A ($15-25M at 25% dilution) only makes sense if we are growing faster than our revenue can fund -- a good problem to have.

**Q: What if sales are slow (bear case)?**
A: Even in the bear case (1-4 new customers/mo, 2% monthly churn), we break even at month 11 having burned only $206K. Post-breakeven, we hire 6 specialists (CSM, sales engineer, compliance counsel, PM, SDR, 2nd sales engineer) -- each with $1K/mo AI tools -- spaced so P&L stays positive. Bear reaches $9M ARR at M48 with 10 total people (2.7x vs static). Bank never drops below $793K. Bear is temporary when margins are 98%.
