# GuardSpine Moat Analysis -- Seven Powers Framework

Based on Hamilton Helmer's "7 Powers: The Foundations of Business Strategy."

## 1. Scale Economies

**Definition:** Per-unit costs decline as volume increases. **Status: FUTURE**
BYOK model means our infra costs are fixed while revenue scales with seats.
Customers pay their own inference costs. At 500+ seats, per-customer cost
approaches zero while price holds steady.

## 2. Network Effects

**Definition:** Each new user increases value for all users. **Status: BUILDING**
Every org publishing tamper-proof evidence bundles strengthens the standard.
Cross-company rubric sharing creates a governance commons. Open-source
connectors (Jira, ServiceNow, Slack) expand the ecosystem per contributor.

## 3. Counter-Positioning

**Definition:** Incumbent cannot copy without damaging existing business. **Status: ACTIVE**
Microsoft, ServiceNow, and GitHub cannot credibly offer third-party governance --
they are the platforms being governed. Open-core vendor neutrality is structurally
required for audit legitimacy. Copying this would undermine their lock-in.

## 4. Switching Costs

**Definition:** Cost incurred by switching to a competitor. **Status: BUILDING**
After 6-12 months, a company accumulates a tamper-proof audit chain that becomes
a regulatory asset. Switching means abandoning chain of custody. Auditors
reference the existing trail -- migration breaks continuity and creates risk.

## 5. Brand

**Definition:** Durable attribution of higher value to identical offering. **Status: N/A**
Not a viable moat at pre-seed. Brand accrues after market presence.

## 6. Cornered Resource

**Definition:** Preferential access others cannot obtain. **Status: CONFIRMED**
Exclusive partnership with Proprioceptive AI (Logan Napolitano) for deterministic
model confidence scoring. MOU signed Feb 24, 2026. 3-year most favored customer
clause. 55 provisional patents on cognitive probes. Exclusive license in AI
governance -- no competitor can access model hidden-state confidence data for
governance applications for at least 3 years. This is the only technology that
answers "was the AI model actually confident, or guessing?" -- governance evidence
vs. governance theater.

## 7. Process Power

**Definition:** Embedded processes enabling lower costs, hard to replicate. **Status: ACTIVE**
AI-native ops: $1K/month API budget replacing 3-4 FTEs. Compounding automation
loop -- each cycle improves rubrics, connectors, evidence quality. Competitors
starting from manual processes cannot leapfrog without rebuilding from scratch.

---

## Summary

**Strongest moats today:** Counter-positioning (structural) + Cornered resource (MOU signed, 3yr exclusive, 55 patents).
**Strongest moats at 6-12 months:** Switching costs (audit chain lock-in) compounded by cognitive probe data lock-in.
**Weakest positions:** Scale economies (need volume) and brand (need time).
