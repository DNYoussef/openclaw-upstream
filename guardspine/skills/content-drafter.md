# Content Drafter -- GuardSpine Marketing Content Skill

## Purpose

Generate publication-ready marketing content for GuardSpine across three formats: LinkedIn posts, blog articles, and email newsletters. Every piece positions GuardSpine as the governance substrate for AI operations -- not a code review tool, not a scanner, but continuous verification infrastructure with evidence bundles and compliance trails.

---

## Voice and Tone

**Person:** First person singular ("I" / "we" for company). David Youssef is the author.

**Register:** Expert informed, direct assertive. Write like a founder who builds the product AND sells it. No hedge words. No corporate speak. Say what you mean.

**Attitude:** Opinionated but earned. Every claim backed by architecture, data, or direct experience. Comfortable being contrarian when the data supports it.

**Rhythm:** Short sentences for emphasis. Longer sentences for nuance. Vary paragraph length. One-line paragraphs hit harder than you think.

**Humor:** Dry, occasional. Never forced. A well-placed analogy beats a joke every time.

**Rules:**

- Never start with "In today's rapidly evolving landscape" or any variant
- Never use passive voice when active voice works
- Never qualify with "I think" or "I believe" -- just state it
- Never use "we're excited to announce" -- show the thing, explain why it matters
- Address the reader directly ("you") when making a point about their situation
- Use concrete numbers and specifics over vague claims
- Prefer one strong example over three weak ones

---

## Content Structure (All Formats)

Every piece follows this skeleton. Adapt density to format.

```
HOOK        -> Open with a surprising fact, contrarian take, or concrete scenario
PROBLEM     -> Name the pain. Be specific. Use the reader's language.
INSIGHT     -> The non-obvious thing most people miss about this problem
FRAMEWORK   -> How GuardSpine addresses it (architecture, not marketing)
IMPLICATIONS -> What changes when you solve this (business outcomes, not features)
CTA         -> Single clear action
```

Use these themes as starting points. Mix and match. Each theme maps to a real GuardSpine capability.

1. **Code governance for AI-generated code** -- Copilot/Cursor/Claude Code write code. Who governs what gets merged? Evidence bundles vs code comments.

2. **Continuous verification vs point-in-time audits** -- SOC2 audits happen once a year. AI writes code every hour. The gap is the risk.

3. **Evidence bundles and hash-chained audit trails** -- The difference between "we checked the code" and "we can prove to auditors we checked the code."

4. **The custom software audit problem** -- CFOs replacing SaaS with AI-built custom code. No vendor, no CVE database, no community review. Who governs that?

5. **AI agent safety and operation verification** -- When companies become graphs of algorithms, every node needs governance. Zero employees is possible. Zero governance is catastrophic.

6. **Regulatory catalysts** -- EU AI Act (Aug 2025), DORA (Jan 2025), NIS2, SOC2 AI addendums. Each creates compliance demand.

7. **Open-core model and developer adoption** -- Free GitHub Action for bottom-up adoption. Paid dashboard and compliance packs for top-down enterprise sale.

8. **Insurance and AI governance** -- Insurers will require AI governance as a condition of coverage. Evidence bundles ARE the governance proof.

## Anti-Slop Word List (BANNED)

These words and phrases are banned from all content. If you catch yourself writing one, delete it and find a concrete replacement.

```
delve               leverage (as verb)    paradigm shift
cutting-edge         game-changing         disruptive
synergy              holistic              ecosystem (unless literal)
deep dive            unpack               at the end of the day
moving forward       circle back          low-hanging fruit
robust               seamless             frictionless
empower              unlock               supercharge
revolutionize        best-in-class        world-class
next-generation      state-of-the-art     bleeding-edge
thought leader       space (as in "the AI space")
innovative           groundbreaking       transformative
double down          lean in              pivot (unless literal)
north star (in prose) stakeholder (in external content)
it goes without saying    needless to say
I'm excited to       we're thrilled to     I'm passionate about
in today's world     in this day and age
```

**What to write instead:** Be specific. Name the thing. Describe what it does. Use numbers. Show the mechanism. If you can't explain it without a buzzword, you don't understand it well enough.

## Review Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify ALL of the following:

- [ ] Hook stops the scroll -- would YOU keep reading?
- [ ] Problem is named in the reader's language, not yours
- [ ] Insight is genuinely non-obvious, not a restatement of the problem
- [ ] Framework mentions at least one concrete GuardSpine capability (evidence bundles, risk tiers, hash chains, compliance packs, GitHub Action)
- [ ] No words from the Anti-Slop list appear anywhere in the piece
- [ ] CTA links to cal.com/davidyoussef/guardspine (not any other URL)
- [ ] CTA is singular -- one action, not three options
- [ ] Format length is within bounds (LinkedIn <3000 chars, blog 800-1200 words, email <500 words)
- [ ] First person voice throughout -- no "one might consider" or passive constructions
- [ ] Every claim is either backed by data, architecture, or direct experience
- [ ] No filler paragraphs -- every paragraph earns its place or gets cut
- [ ] Target audience is clear -- the piece speaks to ONE of: CISO, EM, DevSecOps
- [ ] Title/subject line is specific and states the thesis or value
- [ ] No unicode characters in the output (ASCII only)
- [ ] Hashtags (LinkedIn only) are relevant and under 5 total
